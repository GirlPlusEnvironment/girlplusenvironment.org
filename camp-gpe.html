Throughout camp you'll create content, learn about climate justice, participate in advocacy campaigns, and connect with fellow campers who believe Hot Girl Summer can change the world.

☀️ WHAT IS CAMP GPE?

Camp GPE transforms everyday social media into climate action. Instead of arts and crafts, we're making videos. Instead of camp songs, we're sharing our stories. Instead of capture the flag, we're capturing signatures for climate petitions.

Every two weeks campers receive a brand-new challenge inside the GPE Slack Community. Complete it, submit your proof, earn points for your cabin, and climb the leaderboard.

By the end of summer, you'll have built advocacy skills, created original content, met incredible people, and helped more communities understand why climate action matters.

🌍 WHY WE MADE CAMP GPE

Climate change can feel overwhelming. We wanted advocacy to feel collaborative, creative, and joyful.

Camp GPE makes climate engagement feel like something you want to participate in, not something you have to do.

"Imagine what hundreds of campers can accomplish together. One post can educate someone. One petition signature can influence policy. One story can inspire someone else to speak up."

🗓️ CAMP SCHEDULE

Eight weeks. Four missions. One unforgettable summer.

WEEKS 1-2

BEAT THE HEAT

July 6 - July 17

Extreme weather is becoming more frequent and dangerous. Raising awareness about rising temperatures in Black and Brown communities.

  • ☀️ Sign the Extreme Weather Petition (1 Point)
  • 📱 Complete the Camp GPE Story Sticker (2 Points)
  • 📹 Create a short video (5 Points)

THEME: "Hot Girl Summer starts with protecting our communities."

WEEKS 3-4

TELL YOUR STORY

July 20 - July 31

Personal stories are powerful. Sharing experiences with heat waves, canceled plans, and climate action importance.

  • 📹 Share your climate story
  • 📲 Post using Camp GPE graphics
  • 💚 Encourage signatures for the petition

THEME: "Your story could be the reason someone takes action."

WEEKS 5-6

ENERGY JUSTICE

August 3 - August 14

Why cooling our homes is becoming more expensive and why affordable clean energy is a major justice issue.

  • ⚡ Sign the High Energy Bills Petition (1 Point)
  • 📹 Create a video about utility costs (5 Points)
  • 📱 Share a fact about energy bills

THEME: "Everyone deserves energy that keeps us safe, not stressed."

WEEKS 7-8

FINISH STRONG

August 17 - August 28

Bringing everything together. Celebrating lessons learned and continuing advocacy beyond the summer.

  • 📹 Share your favorite lesson from Camp
  • 📣 Encourage the audience to sign final petitions
  • 💚 Reflect on your advocacy journey

THEME: "Summer may end, but advocacy doesn't."
