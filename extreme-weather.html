CLIMATE EXTREME WEATHER

When the Forecast Gets Personal

Because surviving another record-breaking summer wasn't on anyone's vision board.

Extreme weather isn't just about uncomfortable temperatures, it's about higher energy bills, canceled plans, damaged homes, unsafe working conditions, and communities being pushed to the brink. Black and Brown communities often experience the greatest impacts first because of historic disinvestment, fewer cooling resources, and neighborhoods with less tree cover.

Hot Girls Hate Extreme Weather is helping our community connect the dots between climate change, rising costs, beauty routines, health, and everyday life. Through creator content, educational resources, community stories, and advocacy, we're making climate conversations feel a lot less intimidating, and a lot more relatable.

Because protecting our communities starts with understanding what's happening... and then using our voices to change it.

WHY IS EXTREME WEATHER GETTING WORSE?

Climate change is making many weather events stronger, longer, and more frequent. As greenhouse gases trap more heat in our atmosphere, our planet warms, and that extra heat fuels everything from dangerous heat waves to stronger storms and heavier rainfall.

And yes... the heat melting your makeup before brunch? That's connected too.

WE'RE ALREADY SEEING:

  • ☀️ Longer summers and record-breaking heat
  • 🌧️ More powerful hurricanes and flooding
  • 🌫️ Poorer air quality from wildfire smoke
  • 💸 Higher energy bills as we rely more on air conditioning
  • 💚 More stress on our physical and mental health

WHY DOES THIS MATTER?

STAY SAFE

Learn how to prepare for heat waves, storms, flooding, and poor air quality before they happen.

SAVE MONEY

Understand why your electricity bill keeps climbing and discover ways clean energy and efficiency can lower costs.

TAKE ACTION

Turn climate anxiety into community action by supporting policies that protect the people most affected.

HOT GIRL SURVIVAL GUIDE ☀️💅🏿

"Climate conversations don't have to feel complicated. They can start with everyday moments."

YOUR HOT GIRL WALK

If it's too dangerous to safely walk outside because temperatures remain above 95°F, that's not just "summer being summer." Extreme heat is becoming more common because of climate change.

YOUR NAILS

Extreme heat, UV exposure, and dehydration can affect nail health, and higher temperatures make salon workers and outdoor commuters more vulnerable throughout the day.

YOUR HAIR

Humidity, extreme heat, and poor air quality all affect natural hair care routines. Climate justice includes protecting the environments our beauty routines depend on.

YOUR WALLET

Keeping your home cool costs more every year. Rising temperatures often mean higher electricity bills, especially for families already facing energy burdens.

Climate change is showing up everywhere, and that's exactly why we're talking about it.
