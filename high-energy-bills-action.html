High Energy Bills

Bestie... the math is not mathing.

The Problem

Energy bills are skyrocketing while families struggle to keep the lights on and the air conditioning running during record heat. An aging electrical grid and policies that delay affordable clean energy are making an already expensive problem worse.

The Solution

Tell Congress to support the Energy Bills Relief Act and make it easier to build the clean, reliable energy families need.

  • Modernize and strengthen the electrical grid.
  • Protect families from data centers driving up energy costs.
  • Stop subsidizing expensive fossil-fuel plants ready for retirement.
  • Help households save money through energy efficiency.
  • Expand help for families at risk of losing heat or air conditioning.
