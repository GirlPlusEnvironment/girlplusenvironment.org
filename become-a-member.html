WHAT'S THE GPE NATIONAL COMMUNITY?

The GPE National Community (GPENC) is a free, nation-wide membership network designed for young Black and Brown Women+ looking to learn, engage and connect on climate and environmental solutions. Members get access to vital climate and environmental resources & opportunities, stay updated on events, connect with a community of changemakers, and more!
